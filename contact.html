The Zero Hour Group runs a defense investing newsletter and interview platform built around one audience: retail investors who already follow small-cap and micro-cap defense and drone names closely. IR leads, CFOs, and founders at companies that fit our coverage can reach that audience here, instead of hoping a press release gets read. We offer two formats. A management interview investing session typically works around a specific catalyst - an earnings call, a contract win, a capital raise - when you want context delivered straight to retail investors instead of filtered through a wire story.
