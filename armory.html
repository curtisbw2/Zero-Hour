Shipping, returns and help

            

                
Made to order
Nothing sits in a warehouse. Each piece is printed or embroidered after you order it, so allow 6 to 14 business days for delivery.

                
Shipping
United States only for now. Shipping is shown in your cart and again at checkout before you pay. We email you a tracking link when your order ships. Some pieces are made in different places, so a mixed order can arrive in more than one parcel, each with its own email.

                
Returns
Every piece is made for the person who ordered it, so we can't take returns or exchanges for a wrong size or a change of mind. Check the sizes before you order.

                
Something wrong?
If your order arrives damaged, misprinted, or doesn't arrive, email us within 30 days of delivery with a photo. We'll replace it or refund you.

                
Changes and cancellations
Production starts quickly. Email us right away if you need to change or cancel an order. Once it is being made we can't.

                
Contact
info@thezerohourgroup.com. Payment is handled by Stripe, and we never see your card number.
